# Privacy Policy

**Application:** BLOCKVEXO – Block Puzzle
**Package Name:** com.alam.blockpuzzle
**Developer:** Alam Elhada Fakhereldeen Ahmed
**Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BLOCKVEXO – Block Puzzle collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

BLOCKVEXO is a free puzzle game supported by advertisements.

## 2. Information Collection

The application does not require users to create an account or provide their name, phone number, or email address to play.

However, third-party advertising services may automatically collect certain information, including:

* Device information and operating system version.
* Advertising identifiers.
* IP address and approximate location.
* Application interactions and usage information.
* Diagnostic and performance information.
* Advertising interactions.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

* Provide and maintain application functionality.
* Improve gameplay and application performance.
* Display relevant advertisements.
* Prevent fraud and invalid advertising activity.
* Diagnose technical problems.
* Comply with applicable legal requirements.

## 4. Advertising

BLOCKVEXO uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including:

* Banner advertisements.
* Native advertisements.
* Interstitial advertisements.
* App Open advertisements.
* Rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on applicable privacy requirements and user consent.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## 5. Rewarded Advertisements

Users may voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement to receive 30 minutes of advertisement-free gameplay.

The reward expiration time is stored locally on the user's device.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional and is not required to access the game's main features.

## 6. Local Data Storage

The application stores certain information locally on the user's device, including:

* Game progress.
* High scores.
* Completed levels.
* Daily challenge progress.
* Advertisement reward expiration time.
* Game-related preferences.

This information allows users to continue playing without creating an account.

Users can remove locally stored information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

## 7. Offline Gameplay

BLOCKVEXO supports offline gameplay. An internet connection is not required for its main puzzle features.

However, internet access is required to load advertisements and communicate with advertising services.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not directly sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party advertising service providers as necessary to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws.

Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 9. User Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, the application requests consent before requesting advertisements.

Users may manage advertising preferences through available device settings, Google advertising settings, and applicable consent controls.

Google advertising settings:

<https://myadcenter.google.com/>

## 10. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, they may contact us to request appropriate action.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and preferences are stored locally until the user clears the application data or uninstalls the application.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to their respective data retention policies.

Users may contact us regarding privacy concerns or applicable data access and deletion requests.

## 13. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence, subject to applicable privacy laws and safeguards.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
