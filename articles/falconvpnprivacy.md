# Privacy Policy for Falcon VPN 

Privacy Policy for Falcon VPN



Falcon VPN (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Falcon VPN handles information when you use our Android VPN application.

1. App Overview

Falcon VPN is a VPN application that allows users to connect to the internet through a VPN server. The app uses Android VPN Service functionality to create and manage the VPN connection.

The app does not require users to create an account. Users can continue as guests.

1. Information We Collect

Falcon VPN does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, photos, messages, or precise GPS location.

The app may store limited app-related information locally on your device, including:

* Selected VPN server
* VPN connection status
* Remaining VPN usage time
* Number of rewarded ads watched
* App language preference
* Dark mode preference
* First-use free time status

This information is used only to operate app features and improve the user experience.

1. VPN Service and Network Traffic

When you connect to Falcon VPN, your internet traffic is routed through our VPN server.

Falcon VPN does not intentionally collect, sell, or share your browsing history, websites visited, DNS queries, or the content of your internet traffic.

However, because the app provides a VPN service, network traffic must technically pass through our VPN server while the VPN connection is active. Temporary technical processing may occur for the purpose of providing the VPN connection, maintaining service stability, preventing abuse, and troubleshooting technical issues.

1. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Falcon VPN uses Google AdMob to display ads inside the app. Ads may include:

* Banner ads
* Interstitial ads
* Rewarded ads
* App open ads

Google AdMob may collect and process certain data such as device information, advertising ID, app interactions, ad interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, diagnostics, and ad performance data.

This data may be used by Google to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, prevent fraud, and ensure ad security.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:

<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

1. Rewarded Ads

Falcon VPN allows users to watch rewarded ads to receive additional VPN usage time.

The reward is added only after a rewarded ad is completed successfully. The app may store the remaining time and number of ads watched locally on the device.

1. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

* To provide and maintain the VPN connection
* To manage remaining VPN usage time
* To remember user preferences
* To show ads and rewarded ads
* To improve app stability
* To troubleshoot technical issues
* To prevent abuse or misuse of the VPN service
* To comply with legal and policy requirements

1. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal data.

Some data may be processed by third-party services used in the app, especially Google AdMob, for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the app and service.

1. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the app and service. VPN connections are intended to help protect user traffic while connected.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for using the app lawfully and protecting their own devices.

1. Data Retention

Local app data such as preferences, remaining time, selected server, and rewarded ad status may remain on the user’s device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Falcon VPN does not provide user accounts inside the app.

1. Account Creation and Deletion

Falcon VPN does not allow users to create an account.

Because no user account is created, there is no account deletion process inside the app. Users can delete locally stored app data by clearing app data from Android settings or uninstalling the app.

1. Children’s Privacy

Falcon VPN is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

1. User Choices

Users can:

* Disconnect the VPN at any time
* Choose whether to watch rewarded ads
* Clear app data from Android settings
* Uninstall the app
* Manage ad personalization through Google or Android device settings

1. International Data Processing

Because VPN traffic may be routed through servers located outside the user’s country, data may be processed in countries where our servers or service providers operate.

1. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
